About the Author

Ansh Raj is a young author who believes that big dreams can begin with very small steps. Through his writing, he explores ambition, perseverance, imagination and the journey from an ordinary beginning toward extraordinary goals.

A Story of Possibility

From Nothing to Something is a story built around the idea that where you begin does not have to determine where you finish. It is written for readers who enjoy stories about dreams, determination and achievement.
